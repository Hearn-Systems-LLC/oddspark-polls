## Story

As a Voter or Creator,
I want to see the Tally rendered per the Poll's Visibility Setting,
so that results are visible to exactly the audience the Creator chose.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** an open Poll with Visibility **Live**, **When** anyone with the link opens `/{link}/results`, **Then** the Tally renders as `results-bar` blocks — 34/38px bars on 1px baseline rules, wash fill with a 2px leading edge, label left and `{pct}% · {count}` inside, the leader carrying gold wash/edge plus the `◆` marker, an exact tie withdrawing all gold and `◆` under a `TIED` line (UX-DR3), **And** all counts and percentages are computed server-side from accepted Vote facts via SQL projection (AD-9, NFR-6).
2. **Given** a Voter who has just voted, **When** the confirmation and Tally render, **Then** their own choice appears as a text-only `YOUR BALLOT` line — never a second gold on the surface.
3. **Given** Visibility **After Close** on an open Poll, **When** a Voter who voted (or anyone hitting `/{link}/results` directly) looks, **Then** they see the confirmation/explanation shape — `Results open when the Poll closes — {deadline, local}.` — with the question, no counts, no totals, nothing leaking the result's shape (FR-20, UX-DR19), **And** a Poll with no Deadline (manual-close-only) renders the no-timestamp variant — `Results open when the Poll closes.` — same shape, no hole in the sentence.
4. **Given** Visibility **Creator-Only**, **When** anyone but the authenticated owning Creator requests results, **Then** they see `These results go to the Creator only.` with no counts, while the owner sees the full Tally, **And** every result query authorizes a `ViewerContext` before reading private facts, and creator-only or not-yet-visible responses are served `private, no-store` (AD-21/AR-17).
5. **Given** a Poll with no Votes, **When** the Tally renders, **Then** zero-width bars sit on their baseline rules with labels and the empty-state line (`No Votes yet. Yours would be the first, which is a kind of power.`), and a cold load shows skeleton bars at the correct count with no shimmer, resolving without animation (UX-DR19).

### Architecture Guardrails

- The route is an inbound adapter, `src/modules/results` owns application policy/outward projection, D1 implements outbound ports, and shared domain remains the sole owner of `ResultVisibility`/effective-state semantics. No business rule belongs in Astro frontmatter (AD-1, AD-11, AD-19, AD-23) [Source: `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/architecture/architecture-oddspark-polls-2026-07-29/ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:30-60,194-201,290-346,477-480`]
- D1 Vote facts are authoritative and the Tally is disposable. Runtime multiple-choice counts come from raw accepted facts in SQL, not a stored counter and not a JavaScript fold over all Votes (AD-6, AD-9) [Source: `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/architecture/architecture-oddspark-polls-2026-07-29/ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:109-115,169-175`]
- Authorization before private-fact retrieval is load-bearing. The Results query accepts `ViewerContext`; hidden responses reveal no aggregate, timing/version, manifest, or cache validator. Result/manifests never enter a shared cache; use `private, no-store` for the whole route as the simplest correct rule (AD-21/AR-17) [Source: `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/architecture/architecture-oddspark-polls-2026-07-29/ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:318-327`]
- Use the internal principal user ID supplied by middleware, never Google/GitHub identifiers. Preserve one voter-blind telemetry record and set only internal Poll ID/operation/result metadata; no selection, count tied to a voter, digest, or ballot content enters logs (AD-4, AD-13) [Source: `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/architecture/architecture-oddspark-polls-2026-07-29/ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:80-89,244-252`; `src/middleware.ts`]
- Do not pull forward conditional polling, `representation_version`/ETag validators, reconnect state, live dot, count-up/width animation, leader cross-fade, or chart toggle. These are explicitly Stories 1.9 and 1.10 [Source: `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/epics.md:457-507`]
