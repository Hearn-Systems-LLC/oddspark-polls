## Successes and strengths

1. **AD-3 strategy seam paid off a second time.** The image strategy delegated submission, facts, results, and export to Multiple-Choice and shipped smaller than ranked-choice — no contract version bump, no export-route changes.
2. **Rename-free adoption eliminated a bug class.** Adoption as a pure D1 fact (temp key becomes the immutable adopted key) avoided non-transactional R2 copy ordering entirely.
3. **Adversarial review caught a silent production gap.** The 6.3 review found that the staged deploy config dropped `triggers` and hardcoded the fetch-only entry — the cron handler would never have existed in staging/production. Caught pre-merge, fixed systemically (parsed-config contract test + triggers carried into staged config).
4. **Review follow-through was complete.** ~30 findings across three stories, every one resolved or consciously deferred with rationale recorded.
5. **Epic 5 technical follow-through landed.** T1 (R2 binding audit) was done before 6.1 and cited in its dev notes; the multi-factor gate held on every story.

---

## Challenges and growth areas

1. **Tracker honesty regressed — the Epic 4 P2 rule was missed.** Story 6.3 was marked `done` while unmerged, and six Epic 5 action items sat stale at `open` despite the work being real. Root cause (agreed with Justin): status transitions are manual at the moments of lowest attention; the rule had no enforcement point. Fix: **both** a mechanical merge-path check before `done` and an action-item sweep at every retro/kickoff.
2. **Hexagonal erosion at the route layer.** 6.1 deferred domain-logic-in-route and direct-R2-from-route findings as "design decisions." Consensus: this is debt, and Epic 7's slot builder must not copy the pattern — decision required before 7.1 (P3 below).
3. **Test-quality deferrals accumulating as a class.** Five 6.2 deferrals are all "assert on source text, not runtime behavior" — regexes over component source, no accessible-name verification. Safety-net holes where future refactors will hurt.
4. **Deploy-config drift class.** The cron gap revealed "works in `wrangler dev`, silently absent in deploy" as a previously untested class; a full audit of the staged config against wrangler.jsonc is warranted.
5. **Epic 5 P2 genuinely dropped.** No Ballot Manifest payload baseline was ever recorded. Carried forward as T1.

---

## Key insights and learnings

1. **Rules without enforcement points decay.** The tracker-honesty rule existed for two epics and was still violated; the durable fix is mechanical verification plus a recurring sweep, not renewed discipline.
2. **The strategy port keeps absorbing new poll types for free.** Two epics running, new types land without touching delivery routes or the export contract.
3. **Verify what CI actually deploys, not what wrangler dev runs.** The dry-run checked `wrangler.jsonc`; CI deploys `.deploy/<env>/wrangler.json`. Contract tests must parse the staged artifact.
