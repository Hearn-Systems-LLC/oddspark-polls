# Accessibility Review — oddspark-polls

## Overall verdict

Against the stated pragmatic bar — "keyboard navigation, sensible contrast, alt text on Image Poll images" — the spine's *intent* is unusually strong for a portfolio project: no gestures, no drag ranking, no icon-only buttons, no hover-only affordances, tap targets specified, a real reduced-motion contract, and alt text that blocks publication. The failures are not philosophical, they are arithmetic and specification gaps. Two of the three named bar items are broken in the shipped tokens: the light-mode focus ring sits at **2.25:1**, which is not a visible focus indicator, and the spine's explicit claim that `dim` clears 4.5:1 "in both modes" is false in dark mode (**4.02:1** on void, **3.82:1** on panel) for the token that carries the trust badge, the lost-connection warning, and every piece of metadata in the product.

The deeper structural problem is that **every gold token in `components:` is dark-only**. `DESIGN.md` prose correctly says gold-as-ink is unreadable on paper and introduces `solar-ink-light` for it — then no component references it. An implementer following the token table and swapping `-dark` for `-light` ships a light mode where the selected-option marker, the leading bar edge, the winning round cell, and the focus ring are all between 2.25:1 and 2.42:1. That is one editing pass to fix and it is the highest-leverage change in this review.

## Findings

### Critical

- **[critical]** The focus outline is `2px solid {colors.solar-dark}` (`DESIGN.md § Components → Poll option`; `EXPERIENCE.md § Interaction Primitives`, "Focus"). In light mode gold measures **2.25:1 against `void-light`** and **2.42:1 against `panel-light`** — below the 3:1 a focus indicator needs to be perceivable at all. Keyboard navigation is the first item in the PRD's accessibility bar, and half the product's modes have an invisible focus ring. *Fix:* promote focus to a mode-aware token — `focus-ring-dark: {colors.solar-dark}` (8.04:1 on void, verified) and `focus-ring-light: {colors.solar-ink-light}` `#8A6D10` (**4.57:1** on `void-light`, **4.91:1** on white). No shape change; it stays a 2px square outline at 2px offset.

### High

- **[high]** Gold-as-ink has no light-mode binding anywhere in `components:` (`DESIGN.md § Components`, all of it; the rule exists only in prose at `§ Colors`). Mechanically swapping suffixes yields: selected option marker `◆` **2.25:1**, results-bar `leadingEdgeLeader` **2.42:1**, round-table `winnerColor` **2.42:1**, availability `bestColumnRule` **2.25:1**, input focus underline **2.42:1**, live-indicator dot **2.25:1**. Every one is a load-bearing signal. *Fix:* split the token's two jobs explicitly — `solar-*` is a **fill only** (`#0B0D10` on it verified at **8.04:1**, hover **9.69:1**); every glyph, rule, edge, marker, and underline binds to `solar-ink-*`. Rewrite the component tokens to reference `solar-ink` and add the light twins. One caveat to carry: `solar-ink-light` on `solar-wash-light` over white is **3.91:1** (the availability "yes" `✓` sitting on its own fill) — darken the on-wash glyph to `#7A5F0D` (**4.81:1**) or `#6E560B` (**5.57:1**).

- **[high]** `{colors.dim-dark}` `#67737F` measures **4.02:1** on `void-dark` and **3.82:1** on `panel-dark`, against the spine's stated floor (`DESIGN.md § Colors`, "Contrast floor": "`text`, `dim`, `entropy`, `solar-ink`, and `on-solar`… all clear 4.5:1 against their intended backgrounds in both modes"). The claim is true in light mode (`dim-light` **5.40:1** / **5.80:1**) and false in dark. `dim` carries the trust badge, the live-indicator label, the lost-connection notice, poll-card metadata, comment attribution, table column heads, toggle descriptions, and the results-bar raw count — at 11px and 12px. *Fix:* lift `dim-dark` to `#78848F` (**5.09:1** void, **4.84:1** panel). It is still visibly the middle step of the three-step hierarchy. If the "inherited verbatim, do not tune" constraint on dark values is genuinely inviolable, then the contrast-floor sentence has to be rewritten to exclude `dim`, and every essential-information role listed above has to move to `text-dark` — which is the more invasive of the two fixes.

- **[high]** `{components.results-bar.countColor}` is `dim-dark`, and the count sits *inside* the bar. Over the fills that measures **2.80:1** on `entropy-wash` and **2.50:1** on `solar-wash` (`DESIGN.md § Components → Results bar`). This also contradicts the same section's prose two paragraphs earlier: "Both stay `{colors.text-dark}`; the wash is thin enough that text reads identically over filled and unfilled regions." *Fix:* delete the `countColor` token and let the count inherit `text-dark`, which measures **8.59:1** / **7.67:1** over the two washes — the prose was right and the token was wrong. Differentiate the count from the percentage with the existing `caption` size step, not with color.

- **[high]** Leadership in the Tally is carried by hue alone. `solar-wash` vs `entropy-wash` composited over `panel-dark` is `#3C361C` vs `#252F3C` — a luminance ratio of **1.12:1**; the 2px leading edges are **1.38:1** apart (`DESIGN.md § Components → Results bar`, "Leader treatment"). There is no glyph, no text, no weight change. This directly violates the spine's own Do ("Render every state with glyph *plus* color") and its own Accessibility Floor, whose list of glyph-carried states — availability cells, Toggles, eliminated options, selected options — conspicuously omits the leader. Bar length does not rescue it: the case where leadership matters most is a near-tie, where lengths are indistinguishable, and the spine's `TIED` rule means "no gold" is itself information the reader must be able to detect. *Fix:* prefix the leading bar's value with the `◆` marker already used for a selected option, in `solar-ink`, inside the existing right-hand value cluster — `◆ 47% · 122`. It reuses a system glyph, costs one character of width, needs no box, and makes `TIED` (no `◆` on any bar, label above the group) legible as the absence of a mark rather than the absence of a hue.

- **[high]** Availability **No** (`×`) and **Unanswered** (`·`) are both `faint` on no fill — **2.05:1** dark, **2.48:1** light — distinguished from each other by glyph shape alone, at a ratio the spine itself declares "below a readable contrast ratio… by design" (`DESIGN.md § Components → Availability grid cell`). A voter with low vision cannot tell "I declined this slot" from "I haven't answered yet," which is the difference between a submitted answer and an omission on the one Vote in the product that can be edited. *Fix:* `No` is an answer, not an absence — move `noGlyphColor` to `dim` (**5.09:1** at the corrected value). Keep `Unanswered` at `faint`; it is correctly the null state.

- **[high]** `CLOSED` renders in `label-caps` `{colors.faint-dark}` on the poll card (`DESIGN.md § Components → Poll card`) — 11px, letterspaced, uppercase, at **2.05:1**. This is a Poll's primary status and it breaks the spine's own Don't: "No `{colors.faint-dark}` on text a user has to read." *Fix:* `dim`, matching the metadata line it sits beside.

- **[high]** There is no focus or announcement contract for post-submit outcomes. Voting is a server round-trip on a server-rendered page, and nothing in `EXPERIENCE.md § State Patterns` or `§ Accessibility Floor` says where focus lands or what a screen reader hears after **Counted.**, already-voted, VPN-blocked, bad code, CAPTCHA failure, or rate-limiting. A screen-reader user lands at the top of a reloaded document and must hunt for the result of the most consequential action in the product — including its climax moment. The `aria-live` region specified for the Tally does not help, because it announces *changes* to an already-rendered region, not the state of a fresh document. *Fix:* on every post-submit render, the outcome line is a `tabindex="-1"` element focused on load and is the first content in the main landmark; the document `<title>` leads with the outcome ("Counted — {question}" / "Already voted — {question}"). Add this to `§ Accessibility Floor` as its own bullet alongside "Focus order."

- **[high]** The availability cell's keyboard model is internally contradictory and has no clean ARIA mapping. `EXPERIENCE.md § Accessibility Floor` specifies "each cell is a three-state control reachable by `Tab`, cycled with `Space`, set directly with arrow keys" — but in a grid, arrow keys conventionally *move between cells*, so the two behaviors collide, and `Tab` landing on every cell of a Voters × slots matrix is a large tab-stop count. Three states also have no honest ARIA state: `aria-checked="mixed"` would describe "if-need-be" as partially-yes, which it is not. A competent implementer will guess, and the guesses diverge. *Fix:* model each slot as a `radiogroup` of three named radios — `Yes` / `If need be` / `No` — which the desktop layout already renders as "three discrete targets." `Tab` moves between slots, arrows select within a slot, state is announced natively, and the group's accessible name is the slot's local time. Retire cycle-on-tap on mobile too (see the touch-target finding); a four-step cycle that can land on "unanswered" is both slow and easy to overshoot.

### Medium

- **[medium]** The reduced-motion contract is incomplete. `EXPERIENCE.md § Interaction Primitives` claims "Three, and only three" motion primitives, but `§ Live Results & Motion` defines five timed behaviors: the 480ms width transition, the 180ms spark, the 2400ms pulse, the **400ms count-up**, and the **240ms leader cross-fade**. The reduced-motion clause covers widths, numbers, the spark, and the dot — the **leader cross-fade is in neither list**. *Fix:* correct the primitives count to five and add "the leader's fill and edge change color instantly" to the reduced-motion contract. The change of *state* must survive; only the 240ms interpolation is dropped.

- **[medium]** The signature moment has no screen-reader equivalent. `EXPERIENCE.md § Accessibility Floor` scopes `aria-live` to the aggregate totals line — correct, and the reasoning for it is sound — but the consequence is that a screen-reader user hears "122 votes… 123 votes…" and never learns that the lead changed hands. The product's climax is a leadership change, and it is announced to nobody. *Fix:* leader changes are rare relative to Votes, so announce them on the same polite region: "Pizza now leading, 47 percent." Announce `TIED` the same way when gold is withdrawn. This is additive to the aggregate line and does not reintroduce per-bar chatter.

- **[medium]** The lost-connection notice is a `label-caps` line — 11px, 0.18em tracking, uppercase, `dim` (**4.02:1** at the current value) — and it is the only thing standing between a reader and silently stale numbers (`EXPERIENCE.md § State Patterns`, "Live connection lost"). Separately, the spine says the notice "announces once" but never says reconnection announces, so a screen-reader user who is told the data is stale is never told it is fresh again. *Fix:* render the notice in `caption` 12px `{colors.text-*}` — it is a warning, not structural annotation — and announce reconnection on the same polite region ("Updates resumed"). The `§ State Patterns` row already specifies that reconnection "snaps to current values"; it just needs to say so out loud.

- **[medium]** `void → panel` is not a perceivable step: **1.05:1** in dark (`#101419` on `#0B0D10`), **1.07:1** in light (white on `#F5F7F9`). Three specified behaviors depend on it and none of them will be visible: the results-bar `trackBackground`, the option row's `hoverBackground`, and the cold-load skeleton — "Skeleton bars at the correct count and height, filled to zero width" (`EXPERIENCE.md § State Patterns`), which contradicts the Empty Poll row's promise that "The chart's shape is visible before it has data." *Fix:* don't add a second elevation step — use the system's own device. Give each results bar a 1px `rule` baseline so the bar group reads as ruled bands whether or not it has data; that makes the zero state, the skeleton, and per-option extents all legible with a hairline rather than a tone. For option-row hover, accept that the fill is a near-no-op and let the focus outline carry the affordance (hover is not an accessibility surface; the invisible skeleton is).

- **[medium]** The locked Security Toggle renders as an OFF toggle. `DESIGN.md § Components → Security toggle` specifies the on-appearance "at 55% opacity"; `trackOn` is `solar-wash-dark` at alpha 0.24, so 55% of that is an effective **0.132 alpha → 1.21:1 against void** — an empty rectangle. If the opacity is applied to the whole control (the natural reading of the sentence), the `caption` description drops to **2.00:1**. The Creator looking at a Poll's protections after the first Vote sees the state inverted, which the spine explicitly does not want: "Locked Toggles are never hidden or greyed past legibility; the Creator needs to see what is protecting the Poll." *Fix:* drop the opacity mechanism and give locked its own tokens — full-strength `trackOn`, knob in `dim` rather than `solar-ink` (removing the affordance without removing the state), and the word `LOCKED` in `label-caps` beside the name. The state is then carried by text, not by a tone that reads as its own opposite.

- **[medium]** Option-row semantics are under-specified in the one way that most often goes wrong. `EXPERIENCE.md § Interaction Primitives` says rows are "radio/checkbox semantics" and `DESIGN.md § Components → Poll option` says "The marker is a monospace glyph rather than a native radio or checkbox" — which reads as an instruction to build ARIA widgets by hand, and separately means the marker glyphs (`·`, `◆`, `[ ]`, `[×]`) will be spoken as punctuation ("middle dot, Pizza" / "left bracket, times, right bracket, Pizza"). *Fix:* two sentences in `§ Component Patterns` — the control is a visually-hidden native `<input type="radio|checkbox">` with the row as its `<label>`, which yields roles, names, checked state, arrow-key group navigation, and `Space` for free; the marker is drawn from the label's `::before` and is decorative. The visual spec is unchanged; only the implementation is pinned.

- **[medium]** The rank builder performs a silent bulk state change. Unranking an option "compacts the ranks below it" (`EXPERIENCE.md § Component Patterns`, FR-8) — one activation silently renumbers every control beneath it, and nothing announces the result. A screen-reader user unranks their second choice and has no way to know what the other four options now hold without re-traversing them. *Fix:* each option is a button whose accessible name states its rank and its action ("Pizza, rank 2 of 4, activate to unrank"), and a rank change posts a single polite announcement of the new summary — which the `RANKED {n} OF {total}` line already computes and can serve as the live region.

- **[medium]** 14px Courier Prime is fine for what the voting surface mostly does and a real problem for one part of it. Courier Prime's x-height is roughly 0.42em, so `body` at 14px has the apparent size of about 11px in a humanist sans, and monospace removes the word-shape cues that make sustained prose fast. Option labels, counts, timestamps and codes are short and glanceable — 14px is correct there, and the tabular-figures argument for keeping them monospaced is sound. It bites in three places: the rejection and explanation copy in `§ Voice and Tone`, which is the longest and most consequential prose on the voting page (the IP-check and VPN messages run three sentences); Security Toggle descriptions at `caption` 12px, which are the copy the design says is the whole point of the toggle; and comment bodies. *Fix:* widen `body-lg` 16px from "landing-page prose only" to "any multi-sentence block on any surface," and lift toggle descriptions from `caption` to `body`. The ramp doesn't change, only where its steps are allowed.

- **[medium]** `label-caps` at 11px is right for structural annotation and wrong for the roles it has been given beyond that. Uppercase plus 0.18em tracking removes word-shape entirely, so 11px caps read materially slower than 11px sentence case — acceptable for `QUESTION`, column heads, and field labels, where the reader is scanning for position rather than reading. It crosses into essential information at: the **trust badge** (the product's core trust claim), **`TIED`**, the **lost-connection notice**, **`CLOSED`**, **`RANKED {n} OF {total}`**, **`122 VOTERS · 208 SELECTIONS`** (which exists specifically to stop the numbers looking like a bug), and the timezone line. *Fix:* add one variant — same family, same caps, same tracking, at 12px in `{colors.text-*}` — for labels that carry information rather than structure. The identity is the caps and the tracking, not the 11px; nothing about the silhouette changes and the two variants are indistinguishable at a glance.

- **[medium]** Touch-target contradiction between the two spines: `DESIGN.md § Components → Availability grid cell` specifies `size: 44px`, while `EXPERIENCE.md § Interaction Primitives` specifies "Minimum 48px tap targets on every voting-surface control (option rows, **availability cells**, the vote button)." *Fix:* 48px, and confirm it survives the mobile three-target row — three 48px targets plus a slot label fits a 320px viewport at the 20px mobile margin.

- **[medium]** The Turnstile widget has no accessibility or theming contract (`EXPERIENCE.md § Component Patterns`, "Turnstile widget"). Three consequences: it renders its own light chrome on a `#0B0D10` page unless `theme` is bound to the resolved mode; it is a bordered, rounded, shadowed box inside a system whose first Don't is "No rounded corners"; and its focus ring, contrast, and announcements are Cloudflare's, not yours. *Fix:* bind `theme` to the resolved color mode (including on manual override, not just OS preference), set `appearance: "interaction-only"` so the widget is absent until a challenge is actually required — which matches the spine's own "never blocks reading the Poll" rule — and state in the spine that the widget's chrome is the one sanctioned exception to the shape rules, so an implementer doesn't try to restyle a third-party iframe.

### Low

- **[low]** The disabled vote button is `{colors.faint-dark}` text on transparent — **2.05:1** (`DESIGN.md § Components → Buttons`). Disabled controls are WCAG-exempt, so this is inside the bar, but it is the *first* state a voter sees on a min-selection or ranked Poll, and at 2.05:1 the primary action reads as absent rather than as waiting. *Fix:* `dim` text, keeping the existing `label-caps` hint above it to say what unlocks it.

- **[low]** The Voter Code field is "focused on load" (`EXPERIENCE.md § Component Patterns`). Autofocus drops a screen-reader user into an edit field before the Poll question has been announced, on the one surface where the question is the entire point. *Fix:* drop the autofocus, or keep it and give the field an `aria-describedby` pointing at the question so context arrives with the focus. The field already sits above the options and is hard to miss visually.

- **[low]** Overlay focus return is unspecified. `EXPERIENCE.md § Accessibility Floor` says focus is trapped inside the two confirmations and the code panel and that `Esc` always leaves — but not that focus returns to the control that opened them. *Fix:* one clause: focus returns to the invoking control on close, by any means.

- **[low]** The Security Toggle's hit area is the 40×20px track (`DESIGN.md § Components → Security toggle`), well under 44px. Creator-surface, one user, so the impact is small. *Fix:* the name and description are inside the `<label>`, making the whole row the target.

Computed with the WCAG 2.x relative-luminance formula (sRGB, `L = 0.2126R + 0.7152G + 0.0722B` on linearized channels; ratio `(L₁+0.05)/(L₂+0.05)`). Washes composited by source-over alpha onto the stated backdrop before measurement.

**Two claimed-passing pairs fail:** `dim-dark` `#67737F` at **4.02** / **3.82**, and `solar-ink-dark` — which is defined as identical to `solar-dark` and therefore passes on backgrounds (**8.04** / **7.64**) but is the token every light-mode component silently inherits at **2.25–2.42**.

**The wash claim holds for `text`, fails for `dim`.** `DESIGN.md § Colors` states "labels sitting on a wash use `{colors.text-dark}` and stay readable because the wash is thin." Composited values and measured ratios:

| Wash | Composited | `text` on it | `dim` on it |
|---|---|---|---|
| `entropy-wash-dark` over `panel-dark` | `#252F3C` | **8.59** | 2.80 |
| `entropy-wash-dark` over `void-dark` | `#212A35` | **9.20** | 3.00 |
| `solar-wash-dark` over `panel-dark` | `#3C361C` | **7.67** | 2.50 |
| `solar-wash-dark` over `void-dark` | `#393116` | **8.20** | 2.67 |
| `entropy-wash-light` over white | `#E0E6ED` | **13.04** | 4.61 |
| `solar-wash-light` over white | `#F0E5C3` | **13.04** | 4.61 |

The `faint` restriction is honest. `faint-dark` measures **2.05:1** on void and `faint-light` **2.48:1** on white, exactly as the spine says ("it sits below a readable contrast ratio on void by design"). The restriction is correctly stated; three roles violate it (`CLOSED`, the availability `No` glyph, the disabled button), and those are findings above rather than a problem with the token.

**Other claims checked and confirmed:**

- **Motion is not a photosensitivity risk.** The spark is a 180ms brightness change on a 2px × 34px element — orders of magnitude below the 25%-of-viewport threshold that makes flash rate relevant — and the coalescing rule caps it near one flash per animation window regardless of vote arrival rate. The 2400ms live-dot pulse is an indefinite auto-starting animation, which would engage WCAG 2.2.2, but the reduced-motion contract holds it at full opacity. Both fine as specified.
- **The `+1 day` marker is not color-alone.** It is the literal text "+1 day"; `entropy` (**5.82** dark / **5.70** light) is decoration on top of the words. Correctly built.
- **Eliminated options in the round table are not color-alone.** `faint` plus strikethrough — the line-through carries it, and eliminated rows are intentionally de-emphasized, so the low ratio is appropriate here.
- **Selected options and availability Yes/If-need-be are not color-alone.** Glyph plus fill, as claimed. The gap is `No` vs `Unanswered` (finding above) and the results-bar leader (finding above), not the general rule.
- **The Security Toggle's on/off state is not color-alone** — knob position carries it. The *locked* state is the problem, not the base state.
- **Hairlines at 1.24:1 (dark) and 1.26:1 (light) are below 3:1 and I am not flagging it.** They separate static content rather than identifying controls, and reaching 3:1 would require `rule-dark` near `#5A6670`, which would destroy the identity the whole system is built on. Option-row boundaries are carried by the 48px rhythm and the fixed marker gutter, not by the rule. Accepting this is the right call; it is worth stating explicitly in the spine so it reads as a decision rather than an oversight.
- **No skip link is needed.** The voting page has no navigation chrome above the question, so there is nothing to skip.
- **Several choices exceed the stated bar and should be kept as-is:** alt text blocking Image Poll publication; tap-to-assign ranking instead of drag; no gesture required anywhere; no icon-only buttons; no toasts, no carousels, no hover-only affordances, no auto-advance; "Reducing motion must never reduce information"; and rejection states that keep the ballot on screen rather than replacing it with a blank page. These are the parts of the spine that are genuinely good and none of them need touching.
