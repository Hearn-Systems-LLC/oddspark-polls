- (direction) Why: replace StrawPoll without its $28/mo trust-feature gating — a pain (Justin's own polls) plus an opportunity (public self-service polling for small business owners, web developers, independent entrepreneurs, and millennial Internet nerds) plus a skills-demo mandate; core value is trustworthiness of results
- (capability) CAP-CREATOR-SELF-SERVICE — intent: anyone can sign in with Google or GitHub and create, configure, monitor, close, and delete their own Polls. success: a creator other than Justin completes sign-in, create, share, and vote collection with no operator involvement; unauthenticated creator-route requests denied; one creator cannot mutate another's Polls (FR-1..FR-5, SM-7)
- (capability) CAP-VOTE-MULTIPLE-CHOICE — intent: a voter answers a single- or multi-select Poll in seconds from a shared link. success: out-of-bounds submissions rejected client- and server-side; Tally reports per-option counts and voter count (FR-6..FR-7)
- (capability) CAP-VOTE-RANKED-CHOICE — intent: voters rank options and the system computes a deterministic instant-runoff winner with per-round transparency. success: identical Ballots always yield identical Rounds; anyone can recompute the outcome from the per-Round display and the published anonymized Ballot Manifest (FR-8..FR-10, SM-4)
- (capability) CAP-VOTE-IMAGE — intent: a creator can run a Poll whose options are uploaded images with optional captions. success: images served on voting and results views; format and size caps enforced at upload (FR-11)
- (capability) CAP-VOTE-MEETING — intent: a group finds a meeting time by marking yes/no/if-need-be across proposed slots, each voter in their own timezone, revisable while open. success: slots render in voter-local time; grid ranks by yes count with if-need-be tie-break; a voter revises their own availability without a duplicate Vote (FR-12..FR-14)
- (capability) CAP-VOTE-SECURITY — intent: per-Poll Security Toggles (IP Checks, Session Checks, Voter Codes, CAPTCHA, VPN Blocking) compose independently to match each Poll's stakes. success: concurrent submissions never produce more accepted Votes than the rules allow; code redemption is atomic; toggles tighten-only after the first Vote; new Polls default to Session Checks only (FR-15..FR-19, SM-2, SM-3)
- (capability) CAP-RESULTS — intent: creators and permitted viewers see server-computed, live-updating Tallies, and creators can export raw data. success: a Vote appears in open viewers' charts without reload; Live/After Close/Creator-Only visibility enforced; CSV and XLSX export from the creator surface (FR-20..FR-22)
- (capability) CAP-DISCOVER — intent: anyone can browse open listed Polls; creators opt Polls into the directory; the administrator can delist. success: a new Poll never appears in discovery without explicit opt-in; the directory shows only effectively open listed Polls; delisting preserves ownership, visibility, and Vote data (FR-23, SM-8)
- (capability) CAP-SHARE — intent: anyone can share a Poll from its create-confirmation, voting, and results surfaces. success: a visible text-labelled Share action with native share sheet and copy fallback on all three surfaces; canonical URL never changes; results never gated behind sharing (FR-28)
- (capability) CAP-COMMENTS — intent: a voter can attach one plain-text Comment to their Vote. success: at most one Comment per Vote under the Vote's Security Toggles; Comments follow Tally visibility; creator or administrator can delete (FR-24)
- (capability) CAP-DEMO-SURFACE — intent: a visitor can evaluate the product and its craft via the landing page, a live Demo Poll, and the public repository. success: landing explains the product and links repository, Discover, and create entry; Demo Poll is votable by any visitor with CAPTCHA and Session Checks (FR-25..FR-27, SM-6)
- (constraint) Cloudflare end-to-end (Workers, D1, R2, Turnstile, Better Auth with Google/GitHub); total fixed platform cost stays within USD 0-5/month — any feature breaching the ceiling is out of scope by definition
- (constraint) Voters are always anonymous; no voter accounts, no voter identity beyond secret-keyed digests used solely for duplicate checks, never displayed, exported, or logged
- (constraint) Every new Poll is unlisted by default; listing is an explicit creator opt-in; discovery never exposes a Tally the Poll's Visibility Setting restricts
- (constraint) All Tally computation is server-side from retained raw Vote facts; ranked-choice tabulation is fully deterministic with a published anonymized Ballot Manifest
- (constraint) Voter-facing surfaces are lightweight, server-rendered, and mobile-first; no heavy client framework payload; the product reads as a casual poll card, never a survey form
- (constraint) All Poll and Vote data lives in Justin's own Cloudflare account; no third party holds poll history
- (constraint) Architecture invariants AD-1 through AD-24 (hexagonal modular monolith, D1-owned facts, single Vote transaction, digest privacy, CSRF boundary, outbox media cleanup) bind every implementation; see the adopted ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md companion
- (decision) Non-goals: teams, workspaces, or roles beyond the single Administrator; monetization of any kind; embeds, webhooks, notifications, public API; custom themes, branding, or domains beyond polls.oddspark.dev; PDF export; email invites, automated distribution, or vendor social buttons; StrawPoll data migration; multi-question survey features
- (decision) Success signal: Justin's next real poll runs on polls.oddspark.dev instead of StrawPoll (Phase 1 gate); a publicly shared Poll verifiably withstands a duplicate-voting attempt (Phase 1 gate); a non-Justin creator completes the self-service loop unaided (Phase 1 gate); ranked-choice results are recomputable by hand (Phase 2 gate); a Voter finds and votes in a Poll through Discover
- (question) Creator account deletion: what happens to a deleted creator's owned Polls and their Vote data is unspecified in both PRD and spine; must be decided before public launch (also a Deferred row in the spine)

- (event) Self-validate Pass 2 (preservation): PASS — FR-1..FR-28 map onto the 11 capabilities with no gaps; NFRs land as constraints; PRD non-goals land verbatim; success measures land as the success signal; line-item detail (FR consequences, UX tokens/flows, AD rules) preserved by adopting the PRD, addendum, UX spines, and ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md as companions rather than absorbing them; no wrapper-only drops
